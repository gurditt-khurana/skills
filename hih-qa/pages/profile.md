# Profile page

`/health-record?tab_value=records`, reached via **Profile** in the bottom nav. Header: back arrow, **My Profile** title, upload icon on the right.

Top → bottom:
1. Patient card: name with a dropdown chevron (switch patient), age • gender, avatar with an edit pencil, **View your family** CTA.
2. **Merge duplicate profiles** nudge ("Bring all health records under one right profile for <name>"), dismissible with ×. Shown only if eligible.
3. **Did a family member book a lab test for you?** card with a **Request them to share your reports** CTA.
4. **Your Health Documents:** tiles for Lab Reports, Prescriptions, Radiology scans, Vaccination, each with a count and last-updated date where there's data (e.g. "05 reports • Updated on 23 May'26", "0 scans"), then an **Upload More** CTA.
5. **Health Data:** completion % and last-updated date, edit pencil, a few vitals as pills (e.g. BMI, Current weight), then **+ N more data**.
6. **Talk to your health data:** horizontally scrollable suggested questions (e.g. "How my overall health is performing?", "Explain my lab reports") and an **Ask anything** input → chatbot.
7. **More to explore** list, each row with a subtitle and chevron: Consult a doctor (starting price), Parameter trends (based on N lab reports), Clinician Insights, Your family view (N members), Health recommendations, Request a feature.
8. Footer illustration: "Smart care for the ones you love".
